> [!tip] How to use this wiki (the Feynman way)
> 1. Read one note. 2. Close it and explain the idea out loud in plain words, as if to a 12-year-old. 3. Where you stumble, that's the gap: open the linked notes and fill it. 4. Open the **Test yourself** box. Repeat.
