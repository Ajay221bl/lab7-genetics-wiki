> [!abstract] The big question
> How do the same Punnett-square rules explain human diseases?

## The story in plain words

A disease allele is just an allele. Ask two questions: **where is it** (autosome or X?) and **how many copies does it take** (one = dominant, two = recessive)?

| Type | Copies needed | Examples (study guide Q5) |
|---|---|---|
| [[Autosomal Recessive Disorder]] | 2 | [[Cystic Fibrosis]], [[Sickle Cell Anemia]], [[Tay-Sachs Disease]], [[Phenylketonuria]] |
| [[Autosomal Dominant Disorder]] | 1 | [[Huntington Disease]], [[Marfan Syndrome]], [[Achondroplasia]], [[Neurofibromatosis]] |
| [[X-Linked Trait\|X-linked recessive]] | 1 in males, 2 in females | [[Color Blindness]], [[Hemophilia]] |
| [[Mitochondrial DNA\|Mitochondrial]] | from mother | [[Leber Hereditary Optic Neuropathy]], [[Nonsyndromic Deafness]] |
