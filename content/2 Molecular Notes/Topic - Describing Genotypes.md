> [!abstract] The big question
> What words do we need to describe someone's two alleles and what they look like?

## The story in plain words

Every individual holds two cards per gene. We need words for:
- **Are the two cards the same?** Same = [[Homozygous]] (either [[Homozygous Dominant]] RR or [[Homozygous Recessive]] rr). Different = [[Heterozygous]] (Rr).
- **Which card shows?** The [[Dominant Allele]] shows whenever present; the [[Recessive Allele]] only when it's paired with itself.
- **How do we write it?** [[Allele Notation]]: same letter per gene, capital = dominant.
- **Special names for special individuals**: [[Purebred]] (homozygous, breeds true), [[Hybrid]] (heterozygous offspring of two purebreds), [[Carrier]] (heterozygous for a hidden recessive).
- **How many genes control the trait?** [[Monogenic Trait]] vs [[Polygenic Trait]].

## Build it in order

[[Genotype]] → [[Phenotype]] → [[Homozygous]] → [[Heterozygous]] → [[Homozygous Dominant]] → [[Homozygous Recessive]] → [[Dominant Allele]] → [[Recessive Allele]] → [[Allele Notation]] → [[Purebred]] → [[Hybrid]] → [[Carrier]] → [[Monogenic Trait]] → [[Polygenic Trait]] → [[Gregor Mendel]] → [[Pea Plant]]

> [!question]- Check yourself: A purple kernel's genotype is unknown. Which two words could describe it?
> **Answer:** Homozygous dominant (RR) or heterozygous (Rr).
