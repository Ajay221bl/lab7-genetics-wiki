---
title: "Why codominance and incomplete dominance are different"
type: molecular

---

In incomplete dominance the heterozygote is **in between** (pink: a halfway amount of one product). In codominance the heterozygote shows **both** products fully, side by side (AB blood: both A and B sugars).

A quick test: zoom in. In a pink flower, every cell is light red. In a roan cow (red × white), some hairs are fully red and some fully white. In AB blood, every red cell carries both A and B markers. Both versions are fully present, not diluted.

The sickle cell case shows it depends on what you measure: at the level of the protein, a carrier makes both normal and sickle hemoglobin (codominant); at the level of the person, they're usually healthy (sickle anemia is recessive).

Try it yourself. A carrier's red cells sickle a bit at low oxygen: which pattern is that? Incomplete dominance (an intermediate phenotype).
