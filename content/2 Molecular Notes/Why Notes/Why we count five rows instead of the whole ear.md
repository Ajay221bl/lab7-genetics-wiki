We count five rows because that's a big enough sample (about 100-250 kernels) for a reliable chi-square, while leaving class time for the math.

A sample doesn't need to be the whole population; it needs to be large and fair. Five full rows run the length of the ear, so they aren't biased toward one end. Counting all ~500 kernels would barely change the conclusion and would eat the lab period.

Try it yourself. Why not count just one row? Around 30-50 kernels is small enough that chance wobble could hide or fake a difference.
