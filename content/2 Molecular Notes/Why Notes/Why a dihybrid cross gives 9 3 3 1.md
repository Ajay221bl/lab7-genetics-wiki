A dihybrid cross gives 9:3:3:1 because each gene separately gives 3:1, and when the genes sort independently, you multiply the two 3:1 splits.

(3 + 1) × (3 + 1) = 9 + 3 + 3 + 1. That's literally the arithmetic. ¾ purple × ¾ smooth = 9/16 purple-smooth; ¾ × ¼ = 3/16 purple-wrinkled; ¼ × ¾ = 3/16 yellow-smooth; ¼ × ¼ = 1/16 yellow-wrinkled.

![[Dihybrid Punnett square.png|700]]

*RrHh × RrHh: the 16 boxes group into 9 : 3 : 3 : 1.*

Why can we multiply? Because the color gene and the shape gene are on different chromosomes and sort into gametes independently ([[Law of Independent Assortment]]), so the [[Product Rule|product rule]] applies. If the genes were on the same chromosome close together ([[Gene Linkage]]), the ratio would be off.

Try it yourself. Ear 5: what fraction should be yellow-wrinkled? 1/16 (9 of the 150 counted in the sample).
