Each birth is independent because every child comes from a fresh egg and a fresh sperm, made by meiosis that has no memory of earlier children.

The coin doesn't remember last flip, and the gametes don't remember last baby. So the chance of a girl is ½ every time, and two carriers have a ¼ chance of an affected child every time, no matter what came before.

Study guide Q9: a family with 4 girls. Chance the 5th is a girl? Still ½. Chance of 5 girls in a row **from the start**? (½)⁵ = 1/32. Those are different questions.

Try it yourself. Two carriers have two healthy children. Chance the third is affected? ¼.
