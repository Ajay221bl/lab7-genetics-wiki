We never "accept" H₀ because a small χ² only shows the data is **consistent** with it, not that it's true; other hypotheses might fit just as well, and more data might reveal a difference.

Courtroom again: "not guilty" isn't "innocent". It means the evidence wasn't strong enough. The tray (18:5) fits 3:1 (χ² 0.130) but also fits 2:1 (χ² ≈ 1.39). Neither is proved. You can only say: no evidence against 3:1.

So the lab says "fail to reject" (TA: "accept provisionally"). It's honest about what a test can do.

Try it yourself. A student writes "we proved the ratio is 3:1." What should it say? "We fail to reject H₀; the data is consistent with 3:1."
