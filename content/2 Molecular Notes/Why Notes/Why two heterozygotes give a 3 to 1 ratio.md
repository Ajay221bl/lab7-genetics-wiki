Two heterozygotes give 3:1 because their four equally likely offspring boxes are GG, Gg, gG and gg, and three of the four contain at least one dominant allele.

Genotypes: 1 GG : 2 Gg : 1 gg (the [[1-2-1 Ratio]]). But you can't see the difference between GG and Gg, so in phenotypes GG and Gg merge: 1 + 2 = 3 green, 1 albino.

![[Monohybrid Punnett square.png|700]]

*Gg × Gg: the four boxes give 1 GG : 2 Gg : 1 gg, which looks like 3 green : 1 albino.*

The trap: 3:1 is a probability, not a guarantee. With 23 seedlings you'll rarely get exactly 17.25 : 5.75 ([[Why real counts never match the prediction exactly]]).

Try it yourself. Hh × Hh: what ratio of smooth to wrinkled? 3:1. Genotype ratio? 1 HH : 2 Hh : 1 hh.
