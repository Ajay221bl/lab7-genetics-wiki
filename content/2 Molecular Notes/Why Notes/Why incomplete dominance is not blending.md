Incomplete dominance looks like blending (red × white → pink), but it isn't, because the original alleles come back unchanged in the next generation.

Blending would be like mixing paint: once red and white make pink, you can never get pure red back. But cross two pink snapdragons (Rr × Rr) and you get 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white. The red and white alleles were intact all along, just sharing the plant.

Why pink? One R makes only half the pigment (one cook isn't enough for this dish). Half the pigment looks pink. The **phenotype** is in between; the **alleles** are not mixed.
