Degrees of freedom are categories minus one because once the total is fixed, the last category is forced: only the others are free to vary by chance.

You count 200 kernels on ear 2. If 98 are purple, yellow **must** be 102. Only one number carried any chance; the other was arithmetic. With 4 categories, three are free and the fourth is forced.

![[Degrees of freedom - the forced last count.png|700]]

*With a fixed total, the last count has no freedom.*

Why care? Each free category is a place for chance gaps to add to χ². More free categories → bigger χ² from chance alone → the bar (critical value) has to be higher: 3.841 for df 1, 7.815 for df 3.

Try it yourself. Ear 4 has four phenotypes. df? 3.
