**The big question:** What happens over generations when you follow just one gene?

## The story in plain words

Start with two [[Purebred|purebreds]]: purple RR × yellow rr ([[P Generation]]). RR can only give R; rr can only give r. Every child is Rr, all purple ([[F1 Generation]]). Yellow seems to vanish.

Cross two F₁: Rr × Rr. Each parent gives R or r, 50/50. Four equal outcomes: RR, Rr, rR, rr. Three are purple, one is yellow: **3 : 1** ([[F2 Generation]]). Yellow is back, proving r was hiding, not destroyed.

Want to know if a purple kernel is RR or Rr? Cross it with rr ([[Test Cross]]). Rr × rr gives **1 : 1**; RR × rr gives all purple.

## Build it in order

[[Monohybrid Cross]] → [[P Generation]] → [[F1 Generation]] → [[F2 Generation]] → [[3-1 Ratio]] → [[1-2-1 Ratio]] → [[Test Cross]] → [[1-1 Ratio]]

Try it yourself. You see 52 purple and 48 yellow on an ear. Which cross does that suggest? About 1 : 1, so Rr × rr (a test cross). Confirm with chi-square.
