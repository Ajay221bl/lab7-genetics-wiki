> [!example] The Feynman version
> Each parent is GG, Gg or gg. List every pairing, run a quick Punnett square for each, and see which pairings could ever produce a white seedling.

| # | Parental cross | Expected green | Expected albino |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GG × GG | 100% | 0% |
| 2 | GG × Gg | 100% | 0% |
| 3 | GG × gg | 100% | 0% |
| 4 | **Gg × Gg** | **75%** | **25%** |
| 5 | **Gg × gg** | **50%** | **50%** |
| 6 | gg × gg | 0% | 100% |

Why only six? Three genotypes, and order doesn't matter: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 pairings.

**Only crosses 4 and 5 give both colors**, so only those are worth testing against a tray that has both (TA meeting).

Squares: Gg × Gg → GG, Gg, Gg, gg. Gg × gg → Gg, Gg, gg, gg.
