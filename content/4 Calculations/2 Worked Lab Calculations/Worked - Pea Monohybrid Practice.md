D = purple (dominant), d = white. Parent A DD × parent B Dd.

| | D | D |
|---|---|---|
| **D** | DD | DD |
| **d** | Dd | Dd |

- Genotypic frequency: 50% DD, 50% Dd, 0% dd
- Phenotypic frequency: 100% purple, 0% white

Why: parent A can only give D, so every offspring has at least one D.
