## a. Hypotheses

- **H₀**: the numbers of purple and yellow kernels on ear 1 are not significantly different from the 3 : 1 ratio expected for Rr × Rr.
- **H₁**: they differ because the parents were a different cross (e.g. Rr × rr, 1 : 1).
- **H₂**: they differ for a reason other than the cross (yellow kernels developing less often, counting error).

## b. Chi-square

| Phenotype | O | Expected fraction | E | O − E | (O − E)² | (O − E)²/E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purple | 154 | 0.75 | 150 | 4 | 16 | 0.107 |
| Yellow | 46 | 0.25 | 50 | −4 | 16 | 0.320 |
| **Sum** | 200 | | 200 | | | **χ² = 0.427** |

df = 2 − 1 = 1 → critical 3.841. 0.427 < 3.841.

## c. Results statement

> There was no significant difference between the observed and expected numbers of purple and yellow kernels on ear 1 (χ² = 0.427, df = 1, p > 0.05). We fail to reject H₀: the counts are consistent with a 3 : 1 ratio, so ear 1 most likely came from two heterozygous purple parents (Rr × Rr).
