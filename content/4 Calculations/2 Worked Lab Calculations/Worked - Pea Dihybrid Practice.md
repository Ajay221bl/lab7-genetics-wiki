D = purple, d = white; H = tall, h = short. Parent A DDHH × parent B DdHh.

Gametes: A → DH only. B → DH, Dh, dH, dh.

| | DH | Dh | dH | dh |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DH** | DDHH | DDHh | DdHH | DdHh |

(Every row is identical, since A has one gamete type.)

- Genotypic frequency: 25% DDHH, 25% DDHh, 25% DdHH, 25% DdHh
- Phenotypic frequency: 100% purple tall
