**a.** Autosomal recessive: cystic fibrosis, sickle cell anemia (also Tay-Sachs, PKU).
**b.** Autosomal dominant: Huntington disease, Marfan syndrome (also achondroplasia, neurofibromatosis).
**c.** A = disorder (dominant). Normal male aa × affected homozygous female AA:

| | a | a |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Aa | Aa |
| **A** | Aa | Aa |

100% Aa, 100% affected.
