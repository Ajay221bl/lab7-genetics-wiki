**a.**

| | Hb^A | Hb^A |
|---|---|---|
| **Hb^A** | Hb^A Hb^A | Hb^A Hb^A |
| **Hb^S** | Hb^A Hb^S | Hb^A Hb^S |

- ½ Hb^A Hb^A: normal hemoglobin only; round, flexible cells.
- ½ Hb^A Hb^S: sickle cell trait; both kinds of hemoglobin; usually healthy, some sickling at low oxygen.

**b.** 0% chance of sickle cell anemia: every child gets Hb^A from the normal parent.

**c.** Patterns: autosomal recessive (disease), codominance (both proteins), incomplete dominance (carriers intermediate), heterozygote advantage.
