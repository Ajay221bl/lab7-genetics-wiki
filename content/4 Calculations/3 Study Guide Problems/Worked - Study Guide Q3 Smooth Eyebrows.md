S = pointed (dominant), s = smooth (recessive). Male ss × female Ss:

| | s | s |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | Ss | Ss |
| **s** | ss | ss |

½ Ss (pointed), ½ ss (smooth). **50% smooth eyebrows.** It's a test cross in disguise.
