**a.** X-linked recessive: the gene is on the X; she's color-blind only because both X's carry b.

**b.**

| | X^B | Y |
|---|---|---|
| **X^b** | X^B X^b | X^b Y |
| **X^b** | X^B X^b | X^b Y |

All daughters: normal vision, carriers. All sons: color-blind.

**c.** Males have one X, so one recessive allele shows; females need two.
