**a.** Three phenotypes (curly, straight, wavy) with wavy in between → **incomplete dominance**.

**b.** C = curly, S = straight; CC curly, CS wavy, SS straight. Curly × wavy = CC × CS:

| | C | C |
|---|---|---|
| **C** | CC | CC |
| **S** | CS | CS |

½ CC (curly), ½ CS (wavy), 0% straight.
