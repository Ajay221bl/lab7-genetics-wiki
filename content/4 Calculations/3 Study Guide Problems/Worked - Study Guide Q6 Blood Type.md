**a.** Codominance with multiple alleles: I^A and I^B both show in AB; three alleles exist.

**b.** I^A I^A (type A) × I^B i (type B):

| | I^A | I^A |
|---|---|---|
| **I^B** | I^A I^B | I^A I^B |
| **i** | I^A i | I^A i |

½ type AB, ½ type A; 0% B, 0% O.
