F = freckles (dominant), f = none. Ff × Ff:

| | F | f |
|---|---|---|
| **F** | FF | Ff |
| **f** | Ff | ff |

Genotypes ¼ FF, ½ Ff, ¼ ff (1 : 2 : 1). Phenotypes ¾ freckles, ¼ none (3 : 1).
