> [!example] The Feynman version
> Every box is equally likely, so 'what fraction of kids?' is just 'what fraction of boxes?'

## Steps

1. **Genotype frequency** = boxes with that genotype ÷ total boxes.
2. **Phenotype frequency** = boxes that look that way ÷ total boxes.
3. Convert to percent ([[Calc 01 - Fraction Decimal Percent]]). Check they add to 100%.

## Example: Rr × Rr

- Genotypes: RR 1/4 = 25%, Rr 2/4 = 50%, rr 1/4 = 25% (1 : 2 : 1)
- Phenotypes: purple 3/4 = 75%, yellow 1/4 = 25% (3 : 1)

## Example: DD × Dd (pea practice)

- Genotypes: DD 50%, Dd 50%, dd 0%
- Phenotypes: purple 100%, white 0%

> [!question]- Try it: Ff × Ff (freckles dominant). Phenotype frequencies?
> **Answer:** 75% freckles, 25% none.
