A fraction is a division you haven't done yet. ¾ means 'three divided by four'. Do the division and you get a decimal (0.75). 'Percent' means 'out of 100', so multiply by 100 (75%).

## Steps

1. **Ratio → fraction**: add the parts, put each part over the sum. 3 : 1 → sum 4 → ¾ and ¼. 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 → sum 16 → 9/16, 3/16, 3/16, 1/16.
2. **Fraction → decimal**: divide top by bottom. 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75; 9 ÷ 16 = 0.5625.
3. **Decimal → percent**: × 100. 0.75 → 75%; 0.5625 → 56.25%.
4. **Percent → decimal** (for calculations): ÷ 100. 25% → 0.25.

## Fractions you'll use all the time

| Fraction | Decimal | Percent |
|---|---|---|
| ¼ | 0.25 | 25% |
| ½ | 0.5 | 50% |
| ¾ | 0.75 | 75% |
| 1/16 | 0.0625 | 6.25% |
| 3/16 | 0.1875 | 18.75% |
| 9/16 | 0.5625 | 56.25% |

Try it yourself. 1 : 2 : 1 as percents? 25%, 50%, 25%.
