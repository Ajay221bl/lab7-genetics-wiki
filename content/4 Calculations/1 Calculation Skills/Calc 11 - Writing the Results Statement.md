Write one sentence a stranger could read and know what you tested, what you found, and how sure you are. Then one sentence on what it means biologically.

## Templates (manual p. 125)

**No significant difference:**
> There was no significant difference between the observed and expected numbers of ___ and ___ (χ² = ___, df = ___, p > 0.05). We fail to reject H₀; the data are consistent with a ___ ratio, so the parents were most likely ___.

**Significant difference:**
> There were significantly more ___ and fewer ___ than expected for a ___ ratio (χ² = ___, df = ___, p < 0.05). We reject H₀; the parents were probably not ___.

TA meeting checklist: the hypothesis, the actual numbers, the meaning, **in your own words**.
