The symbol Σ (the Greek capital letter sigma) means "add up all of these".

When you see Σ (O − E)²/E, read it as: "work out (O − E)²/E for every category, then add them all together". If you have two categories (green and albino), Σ means two numbers added. If you have four kernel types, it means four numbers added.

Why use a symbol instead of writing a + b + c? Because the number of things to add changes. Σ is a promise to add however many there are, so one formula works for the seedlings (2 categories), the ears (2 or 4) and next week's beads (5).

In the manual's chi-square table, Σ is the box at the bottom of the last column labeled "Sum =".

The trap: summing the wrong column. Σ applies to the (O − E)²/E column, not the O − E column. The O − E column always adds to zero, which is a handy check but not your answer.

Try it yourself. The last column reads 0.033 and 0.098. What is Σ? 0.131 (0.130 with unrounded numbers). That's χ².
