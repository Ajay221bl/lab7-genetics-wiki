The law of large numbers says that as you repeat a chance event more times, the observed share gets closer to the true probability.

It doesn't mean chance "evens out" by correcting itself (coins have no memory). The early wobble just becomes a smaller and smaller fraction of a growing total.

Try it yourself. After 10 flips you have 7 heads. Will the next flips favor tails to fix it? No. The 2 extra heads just matter less as the total grows.
