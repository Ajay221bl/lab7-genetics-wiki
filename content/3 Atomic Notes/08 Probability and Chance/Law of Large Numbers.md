> [!abstract] In one sentence
> The law of large numbers says that as you collect more independent observations, the observed proportion gets closer and closer to the true probability.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Flip enough coins and the share of heads settles near ½.

## Build it from first principles

Toss a fair coin 10 times and 7 heads is unremarkable; toss it 1,000 times and 700 heads would be shocking. Same for kernels: one table's ratio wanders, the class's ratio settles near the true 3 : 1.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Will the class ratio or a table ratio usually be closer to 3 : 1?
> **Answer:** The class ratio (bigger sample).
