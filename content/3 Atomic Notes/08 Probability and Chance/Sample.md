A sample is the part of the population you actually count.

Like tasting a spoonful to judge the whole pot. Your tray of seedlings, your five rows of kernels: samples. The bigger and fairer the spoonful, the better it represents the pot.

Try it yourself. Why count five rows instead of one? One row is too small a spoonful ([[Why we count five rows instead of the whole ear]]).
