> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A sample is the subset of a population that you actually observe or count.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> The spoonful you taste to judge the whole pot.

## Build it from first principles

Slide 20: 'Samples = a subset of larger population.' In Lab 7, five rows of kernels are a sample of the whole ear; one table's 24 seedlings are a sample of all offspring of that cross; the class total is a bigger sample.

The TA meeting framed 'count five rows, not the whole cob' as a lesson in sampling a population.

> [!question]- Test yourself: What is your sample when counting an ear?
> **Answer:** The kernels in five rows.
