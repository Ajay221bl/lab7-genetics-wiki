A percent is a proportion written out of 100: multiply by 100 and add %.

¾ = 0.75 = 75%. "Per cent" literally means "per hundred".

The trap in this lab: the chi-square test needs **counts**, not percentages ([[Why chi-square needs counts not percentages]]).

Try it yourself. 0.05 as a percent? 5%. That's the lab's [[Level of Significance]].
