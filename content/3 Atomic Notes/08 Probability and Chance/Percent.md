> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A percent is a proportion expressed out of 100: divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Percent means 'per hundred'.

## Build it from first principles

¾ → 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75 → × 100 = **75%**. The TA meeting noted many students struggle with percents; see [[Calc 01 - Fraction Decimal Percent]].

> [!question]- Test yourself: ¼ as a percent?
> **Answer:** 25%.
