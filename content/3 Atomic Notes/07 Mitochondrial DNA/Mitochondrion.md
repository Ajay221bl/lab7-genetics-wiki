A mitochondrion (plural mitochondria) is the organelle that burns food with oxygen to make ATP, the cell's energy currency.

It's the cell's power plant. And it's a strange one: it has its own small loop of DNA, a double membrane, and it divides by itself, all clues that it was once a free-living bacterium ([[Endosymbiotic Theory]]).

Try it yourself. What do mitochondria make? ATP.
