> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A mitochondrion (plural mitochondria) is the organelle that uses oxygen to break down food and make ATP, the cell's energy currency.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> The cell's power plant, which was once an independent bacterium.

## Build it from first principles

Often called the cell's power plant. Mitochondria are unusual: they have their own small circular [[Mitochondrial DNA|DNA]], a double membrane, and they divide on their own, all traces of their origin as bacteria ([[Endosymbiotic Theory]]).

> [!question]- Test yourself: What does a mitochondrion make?
> **Answer:** ATP.
