> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A eukaryote is an organism whose cells keep their DNA in a nucleus and have membrane-bound organelles such as mitochondria; plants, animals and fungi are eukaryotes.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> A cell with rooms: a nucleus and organelles.

## Build it from first principles

Both corn and humans are eukaryotes, with nuclear chromosomes (inherited from both parents) plus mitochondria (from the mother).

> [!question]- Test yourself: Is corn a eukaryote?
> **Answer:** Yes.
