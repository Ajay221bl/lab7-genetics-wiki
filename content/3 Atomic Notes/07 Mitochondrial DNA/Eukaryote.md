A eukaryote is an organism whose cells keep their DNA in a nucleus and contain organelles like mitochondria; plants, animals and fungi are eukaryotes.

A cell with rooms. Corn and humans are eukaryotes: nuclear chromosomes from both parents, plus mitochondria from the mother.

Try it yourself. Is corn a eukaryote? Yes.
