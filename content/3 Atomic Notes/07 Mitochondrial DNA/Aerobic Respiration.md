> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Aerobic respiration is the oxygen-using breakdown of food molecules in mitochondria that releases energy and stores it in ATP.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Burning food slowly with oxygen and banking the energy as ATP.

## Build it from first principles

*Aerobic* = using oxygen. It's why we breathe. 13 of the 37 mtDNA genes code for parts of this process. Cells with big energy needs (nerve, muscle) suffer most when it fails, which is why mtDNA diseases often hit the eyes, ears and brain.

> [!question]- Test yourself: What does 'aerobic' mean?
> **Answer:** Using oxygen.
