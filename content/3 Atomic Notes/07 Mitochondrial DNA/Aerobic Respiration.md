Aerobic respiration is the oxygen-using breakdown of food in mitochondria, which releases energy and stores it as ATP.

"Aerobic" means with oxygen; it's why we breathe. 13 of the 37 mtDNA genes help run it. Tissues that use the most energy (nerves, muscles, eyes, ears) suffer first when it fails, which is why mtDNA diseases hit those tissues.

Try it yourself. Why does an mtDNA disease often affect vision? The optic nerve uses a lot of energy.
