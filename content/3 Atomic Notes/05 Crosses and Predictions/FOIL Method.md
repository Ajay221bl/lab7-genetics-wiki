> [!abstract] In one sentence
> The FOIL method (First, Outer, Inner, Last) is a shortcut for listing the four gametes of a dihybrid such as RrHh: RH, Rh, rH, rh.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> The algebra trick (a+b)(c+d) lists all four combinations: the same trick lists all four dihybrid gametes.

## Build it from first principles

Write the genotype as two pairs: (R r)(H h). Then:
- **F**irst: R with H → **RH**
- **O**uter: R with h → **Rh**
- **I**nner: r with H → **rH**
- **L**ast: r with h → **rh**

Check: every gamete has exactly one color letter and one shape letter. Recommended in the TA meeting because gamete-writing is the most common error.

For rrhh, FOIL gives rh four times: only one gamete type.

> [!question]- Test yourself: FOIL the gametes of RrHh.
> **Answer:** RH, Rh, rH, rh.
