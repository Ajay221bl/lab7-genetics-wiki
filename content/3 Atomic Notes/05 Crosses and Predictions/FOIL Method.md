FOIL (First, Outer, Inner, Last) is a shortcut for listing the four gametes of a double heterozygote like RrHh: RH, Rh, rH, rh.

You may remember FOIL from algebra, for multiplying (a + b)(c + d). Write the genotype as two pairs, (R r)(H h), and pair them up:

- **F**irst: R with H → RH
- **O**uter: R with h → Rh
- **I**nner: r with H → rH
- **L**ast: r with h → rh

Check every result has one color letter and one shape letter. The TA meeting recommends FOIL because writing gametes is the most common mistake in the dihybrid crosses.

For rrhh, FOIL gives rh four times: only one gamete type.

Try it yourself. FOIL the gametes of DdHh. DH, Dh, dH, dh.
