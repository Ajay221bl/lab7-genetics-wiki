Self-pollination is fertilizing a plant with its own pollen, which crosses a plant with itself.

Selfing an Rr plant is the same as the cross Rr × Rr: you get 3 purple : 1 yellow. Selfing an RR plant gives only RR, which is how breeders make and keep [[Purebred|purebred]] lines. Mendel used self-pollination in peas to make his purebreds.

Try it yourself. A corn plant is selfed and its ear shows 3 purple : 1 yellow. What's the plant's genotype? Rr.
