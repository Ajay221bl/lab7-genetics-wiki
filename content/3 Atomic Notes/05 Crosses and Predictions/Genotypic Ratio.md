> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A genotypic ratio compares how many offspring of each genotype a cross is expected to produce, e.g. 1 RR : 2 Rr : 1 rr.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> A headcount of offspring by their hidden letters.

## Build it from first principles

Count the Punnett square boxes for each distinct genotype. RR, Rr and rR are separate boxes but Rr and rR are the same genotype, so Rr × Rr gives 1 : 2 : 1.

The genotypic and phenotypic ratios differ whenever two genotypes look the same (complete dominance).

> [!question]- Test yourself: Genotypic ratio of Rr × Rr?
> **Answer:** 1 RR : 2 Rr : 1 rr.
