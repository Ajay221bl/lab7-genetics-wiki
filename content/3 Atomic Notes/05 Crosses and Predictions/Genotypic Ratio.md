A genotypic ratio compares how many offspring of each genotype a cross is expected to give, like 1 RR : 2 Rr : 1 rr.

Count the Punnett boxes for each genotype. Rr and rR are separate boxes but the same genotype, which is why Rr gets 2.

Try it yourself. Genotypic ratio of Rr × rr? 1 Rr : 1 rr.
