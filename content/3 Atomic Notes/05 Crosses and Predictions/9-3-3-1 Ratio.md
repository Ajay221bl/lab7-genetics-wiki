The 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ratio is the phenotype ratio from crossing two double heterozygotes (RrHh × RrHh) when the genes assort independently.

Out of 16: **9** dominant for both (purple smooth), **3** dominant for color only (purple wrinkled), **3** dominant for shape only (yellow smooth), **1** recessive for both (yellow wrinkled). The fast way to see it: ¾ × ¾ = 9/16, ¾ × ¼ = 3/16, ¼ × ¾ = 3/16, ¼ × ¼ = 1/16 ([[Why a dihybrid cross gives 9 3 3 1]]).

Lab 7: ear 5. Four categories means df = 3 and a critical value of 7.815.

Try it yourself. 160 kernels from RrHh × RrHh. Expected purple smooth? 160 × 9/16 = 90.
