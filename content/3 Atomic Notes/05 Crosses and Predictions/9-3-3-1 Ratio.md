> [!abstract] In one sentence
> The 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ratio is the phenotype ratio of a dihybrid cross between two double heterozygotes (RrHh × RrHh) when the genes assort independently.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Two independent 3 : 1 coins multiplied together: ¾ × ¾ = 9/16 both dominant, down to ¼ × ¼ = 1/16 both recessive.

## Build it from first principles

Out of 16: **9** both dominant (purple smooth), **3** first dominant only (purple wrinkled), **3** second dominant only (yellow smooth), **1** both recessive (yellow wrinkled).

Shortcut by multiplying the 3 : 1 ratios: (¾ + ¼) × (¾ + ¼) → 9/16, 3/16, 3/16, 1/16. See [[Product Rule]].

Lab 7: Corn Ear 5. Its chi-square has 4 categories, so df = 3 and the critical value is 7.815.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Fraction of yellow wrinkled from RrHh × RrHh?
> **Answer:** 1/16.
