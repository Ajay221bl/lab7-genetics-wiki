A phenotypic ratio compares how many offspring of each phenotype a cross is expected to give, like 3 purple : 1 yellow.

Group boxes by what they look like. Under complete dominance RR and Rr both look purple, so 1 : 2 : 1 genotypes collapse into 3 : 1 phenotypes. You test **phenotypic** ratios with chi-square, because phenotypes are what you can count.

Try it yourself. Under incomplete dominance, what is the phenotypic ratio of Rr × Rr? 1 : 2 : 1, the same as the genotypes.
