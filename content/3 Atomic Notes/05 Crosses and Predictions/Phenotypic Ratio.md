> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A phenotypic ratio compares how many offspring of each phenotype a cross is expected to produce, e.g. 3 purple : 1 yellow.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> A headcount of offspring by what they look like.

## Build it from first principles

Group Punnett boxes by what they look like. Under complete dominance RR and Rr both look purple, so 1 : 2 : 1 genotypes collapse to 3 : 1 phenotypes.

You test **phenotypic** ratios with chi-square, because phenotypes are what you can count.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Phenotypic ratio of Rr × Rr?
> **Answer:** 3 purple : 1 yellow.
