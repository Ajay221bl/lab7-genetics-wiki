> [!abstract] In one sentence
> The 1 : 1 ratio is expected when a heterozygote is crossed with a homozygous recessive (Rr × rr): half dominant phenotype, half recessive.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Half and half, like a fair coin: the heterozygous parent's coin flip shows directly.

## Build it from first principles

Rr × rr → Rr, Rr, rr, rr. The rr parent can only give r, so the offspring's phenotype simply shows which allele the Rr parent gave.

Lab 7: seedlings if the parents were Gg × gg; Corn Ear 2 (the test cross).

> [!question]- Test yourself: Which ear shows 1 : 1?
> **Answer:** Ear 2 (Rr × rr).
