The 1 : 1 ratio is expected when a heterozygote is crossed with a homozygous recessive (Rr × rr): half dominant phenotype, half recessive.

The rr parent can only give r, so each child's phenotype shows directly whichever allele the Rr parent gave, and that's a coin flip.

Lab 7: the seedlings if the parents were Gg × gg; ear 2.

Try it yourself. 23 seedlings, hypothesis 1 : 1. Expected numbers? 11.5 and 11.5.
