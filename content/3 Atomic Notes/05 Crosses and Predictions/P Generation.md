> [!abstract] In one sentence
> The P (parental) generation is the pair of parents that starts a breeding experiment, classically two purebred individuals such as RR × rr.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> The founding grandparents of the experiment, usually two purebreds.

## Build it from first principles

Manual p. 122: 'Homozygous parents are referred to as the parental-generation, or the P-generation.'

Corn Ear crosses 1 and 4 on pp. 130-131 start from a P generation: homozygous purple (RR) × homozygous yellow (rr), and homozygous smooth (HH) × homozygous wrinkled (hh).

> [!question]- Test yourself: Typical P cross for kernel color?
> **Answer:** RR × rr.
