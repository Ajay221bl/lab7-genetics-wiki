The P (parental) generation is the pair of parents that starts a breeding experiment, classically two purebreds like RR × rr.

The manual (p. 122): "Homozygous parents are referred to as the parental-generation, or the P-generation." Ears 1 and 3 start from a P cross: homozygous purple × homozygous yellow (RR × rr), and homozygous smooth × homozygous wrinkled (HH × hh).

Try it yourself. What are all the F₁ offspring of HH × hh? Hh, all smooth.
