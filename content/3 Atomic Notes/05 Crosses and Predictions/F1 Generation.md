> [!abstract] In one sentence
> The F₁ (first filial) generation is the offspring of the P generation; from two different purebreds, every F₁ is heterozygous and identical.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> The kids of the founders: all mixes (Rr), all looking like the dominant parent.

## Build it from first principles

*Filial* is from Latin *filius/filia*, son/daughter. RR × rr → all **Rr** (100% purple). The recessive trait seems to disappear in F₁, which is what puzzled people before Mendel.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Phenotype of all F₁ from RR × rr?
> **Answer:** Purple.
