The F₁ (first filial) generation is the offspring of the P generation; from two different purebreds, every F₁ is heterozygous and they all look alike.

"Filial" comes from the Latin for son and daughter. RR × rr: one parent can only give R, the other only r, so every child is Rr, all purple. The yellow trait seems to vanish. That disappearance puzzled people for centuries, until the F₂ showed it was only hiding.

Try it yourself. All F₁ from RR × rr are purple. Did the r allele disappear? No: every F₁ carries it, hidden by R.
