The 3 : 1 ratio is the phenotype ratio when two heterozygotes are crossed under complete dominance: ¾ dominant, ¼ recessive.

Why 3 and 1? Rr × Rr gives four equally likely boxes: RR, Rr, Rr, rr. Three contain at least one R, so they look dominant. One doesn't ([[Why two heterozygotes give a 3 to 1 ratio]]).

Lab 7: the seedlings if the parents were Gg × Gg; ear 1 (purple : yellow); ear 3 (smooth : wrinkled).

Try it yourself. 200 kernels from Rr × Rr: how many yellow would you expect? 200 × ¼ = 50.
