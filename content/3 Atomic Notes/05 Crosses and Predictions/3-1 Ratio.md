> [!abstract] In one sentence
> The 3 : 1 ratio is the phenotype ratio expected when two heterozygotes are crossed under complete dominance (¾ dominant, ¼ recessive).

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Three of four boxes contain at least one dominant letter, so 3 of 4 offspring look dominant.

## Build it from first principles

Where it comes from: Rr × Rr → RR + Rr + Rr + rr. Three boxes contain at least one R → dominant phenotype.

Lab 7: green/albino seedlings if the parents were Gg × Gg; Corn Ear 1 (purple/yellow); Corn Ear 3 (smooth/wrinkled).

> [!question]- Test yourself: Which cross gives 3 : 1?
> **Answer:** Two heterozygotes (Rr × Rr) under complete dominance.
