> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Genotype frequency is the fraction (or percent) of offspring expected to have each genotype, found by dividing that genotype's Punnett boxes by the total boxes.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> The share of the 'pie' each genotype gets.

## Build it from first principles

Rr × Rr: RR is 1 of 4 boxes → ¼ = 25%; Rr is 2 of 4 → ½ = 50%; rr → ¼ = 25%. The frequencies always add up to 1 (100%).

The manual's tables ask for 'Predicted Genotypic Frequency' after each Punnett square.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Frequency of Rr from Rr × Rr?
> **Answer:** ½ (50%).
