Genotype frequency is the fraction (or percent) of offspring expected to have a given genotype: its number of Punnett boxes divided by the total.

Rr × Rr: RR is 1 of 4 boxes, ¼ or 25%; Rr is 2 of 4, ½ or 50%; rr is ¼ or 25%. They always add to 100%. The manual asks for "Predicted Genotypic Frequency" under every square.

Try it yourself. Genotype frequencies of DD × Dd? 50% DD, 50% Dd.
