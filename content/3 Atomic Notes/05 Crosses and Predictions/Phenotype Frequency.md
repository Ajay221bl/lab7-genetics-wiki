Phenotype frequency is the fraction (or percent) of offspring expected to show a given phenotype, found by grouping Punnett boxes that look alike.

Rr × Rr: purple boxes are RR, Rr, Rr, so 3 of 4, ¾ or 75%. Yellow is ¼ or 25%.

In the chi-square table this is the "Expected Frequency" column. Multiply it by your total count to get the expected **number**, E ([[Calc 06 - Expected Counts]]).

Try it yourself. Phenotype frequencies of Rr × rr? 50% purple, 50% yellow.
