> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A Y-linked trait is controlled by a gene on the Y chromosome, so it occurs only in males and passes from father to son.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Y genes are a family heirloom passed only father to son.

## Build it from first principles

The Y carries few genes. The most important triggers male development. Manual p. 122 mentions a gene that 'produces very hairy ears (in males only!)'. (That example is old and disputed, but it illustrates the rule.)

> [!question]- Test yourself: Can a woman have a Y-linked trait?
> **Answer:** No; she has no Y.
