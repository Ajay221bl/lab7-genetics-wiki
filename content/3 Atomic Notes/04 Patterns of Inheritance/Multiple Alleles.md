Multiple alleles means a gene has more than two versions in the population, even though each individual still carries only two.

Slide 16: the blood-type gene has three alleles, Iᴬ, Iᴮ and i. Think of a menu with three dishes where everyone orders exactly two portions. Six combinations are possible (IᴬIᴬ, Iᴬi, IᴮIᴮ, Iᴮi, IᴬIᴮ, ii), giving four blood types.

Don't confuse it with [[Polygenic Trait|polygenic]]: multiple alleles is one gene with several versions; polygenic is several genes.

Try it yourself. How many alleles of the blood-type gene does one person carry? Two.
