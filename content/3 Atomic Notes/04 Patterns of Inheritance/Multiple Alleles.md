> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Multiple alleles means a gene has more than two alleles in the population, although each individual still carries only two.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> A menu with three options where each person can only order two.

## Build it from first principles

Slide 16: the blood-type gene has three alleles: **I^A, I^B and i**. Six genotypes are possible (I^A I^A, I^A i, I^B I^B, I^B i, I^A I^B, ii) giving four blood types.

Don't confuse this with [[Polygenic Trait|polygenic]] (many genes) — multiple alleles is one gene with many versions.

> [!question]- Test yourself: How many alleles does the ABO gene have?
> **Answer:** Three: I^A, I^B, i.
