> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Complete dominance is the pattern in which one allele completely masks the other, so heterozygotes look exactly like the dominant homozygote.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> One allele is a loud singer, the other quiet: in a duet you only hear the loud one.

## Build it from first principles

Slide 6: 'A dominant gene completely hides the effect of a recessive one.' BB and Bb are brown-eyed; only bb is blue (or green, slide 10).

Fingerprint: only **two** phenotypes; the [[F2 Generation|F₂]] gives a **3 : 1** phenotype ratio, though the genotype ratio is 1 : 2 : 1.

Lab 7 examples: purple over yellow kernels, smooth over wrinkled, green over albino seedlings, freckles over none.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Phenotype ratio of Rr × Rr under complete dominance?
> **Answer:** 3 : 1.
