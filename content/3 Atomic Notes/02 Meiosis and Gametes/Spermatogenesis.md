> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Spermatogenesis is the making of sperm by meiosis; it produces four small, streamlined sperm from each starting cell.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Sperm-making is mass production of lightweight couriers: four equal, stripped-down cells from each start.

## Build it from first principles

Unlike [[Oogenesis|oogenesis]], spermatogenesis divides equally and then strips each cell down to a nucleus, a midpiece of a few mitochondria and a tail. Very little cytoplasm reaches the egg, which is part of why mitochondria are inherited maternally.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Why do sperm contribute few mitochondria?
> **Answer:** They are stripped down to nucleus, a few mitochondria and a tail.
