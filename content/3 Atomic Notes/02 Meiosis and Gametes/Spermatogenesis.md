Spermatogenesis is the making of sperm by meiosis: four equal, small, streamlined cells from each starting cell.

Unlike oogenesis, it divides evenly and then strips each cell down to a nucleus, a few mitochondria and a tail: mass production of lightweight couriers. Very little cytoplasm reaches the egg, which is part of why mitochondria are inherited from the mother.

Try it yourself. How many sperm come from one starting cell? Four.
