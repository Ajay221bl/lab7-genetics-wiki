> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Red-green color blindness is an X-linked recessive trait in which one type of color-detecting cone in the eye doesn't work normally.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> One of the three color sensors in the eye is off, and the gene for it sits on the X.

## Build it from first principles

Slides 13-14 and study guide Q7. About 8% of men but under 1% of women are red-green color-blind, because men need one X^b and women need two.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Color-blind woman × normal man: what about their sons?
> **Answer:** All sons are color-blind (X^b Y).
