Hemoglobin is the protein that packs red blood cells and carries oxygen around the body.

Think of it as an oxygen taxi. The normal allele is Hbᴬ. The sickle allele, Hbˢ, changes just one [[Amino Acid|amino acid]] in the protein, and that one change makes the taxis stick together when oxygen is low.

Try it yourself. How big is the difference between normal and sickle hemoglobin? One amino acid.
