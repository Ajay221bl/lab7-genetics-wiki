> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Hemoglobin is the oxygen-carrying protein that packs red blood cells.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Hemoglobin is the oxygen taxi inside red blood cells.

## Build it from first principles

The sickle-cell allele (Hb^S) changes a single amino acid in hemoglobin. At low oxygen, sickle hemoglobin sticks together into long rods that bend the cell into a stiff crescent. Normal allele = Hb^A.

> [!question]- Test yourself: How many amino acids differ in sickle hemoglobin?
> **Answer:** One.
