Anemia is a shortage of healthy red blood cells or hemoglobin, so tissues get too little oxygen.

Too few oxygen taxis on the road. In sickle cell anemia, sickled cells break down after about 10-20 days instead of the normal 120, and the body can't replace them fast enough.

Try it yourself. Why does sickle cell disease cause anemia? Sickled red cells are destroyed early.
