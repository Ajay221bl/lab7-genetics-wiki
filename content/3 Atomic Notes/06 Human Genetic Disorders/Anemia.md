> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Anemia is a shortage of healthy red blood cells (or hemoglobin), so the body's tissues get too little oxygen.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Too few oxygen taxis on the road.

## Build it from first principles

In sickle cell anemia, sickled cells break down after about 10-20 days instead of about 120, so the body can't replace them fast enough.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Why does sickle cell cause anemia?
> **Answer:** Sickled red cells break down early.
