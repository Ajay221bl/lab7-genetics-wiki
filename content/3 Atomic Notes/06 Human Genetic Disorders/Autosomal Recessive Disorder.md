> [!abstract] In one sentence
> An autosomal recessive disorder is caused by an allele on an autosome that must be present in two copies (aa) for the person to be affected.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> You need two broken copies to be sick; with one, a working copy covers for you.

## Build it from first principles

Two unaffected [[Carrier|carriers]] (Aa × Aa) have a **¼ chance per child** of an affected child. Each child is an independent event: having one affected child doesn't change the odds for the next.

Examples (study guide Q5a): [[Cystic Fibrosis]], [[Sickle Cell Anemia]], [[Tay-Sachs Disease]], [[Phenylketonuria]].

> [!question]- Test yourself: Name two autosomal recessive disorders.
> **Answer:** Cystic fibrosis, sickle cell anemia (also Tay-Sachs, PKU).
