An autosomal recessive disorder is caused by an allele on an autosome that has to be present in two copies (aa) for someone to be affected.

One working copy covers for the broken one, so carriers (Aa) are healthy. Two carriers, Aa × Aa, have a ¼ chance with each child of an affected child. "Each child" matters: every birth is a fresh draw.

Examples (study guide Q5a): [[Cystic Fibrosis]], [[Sickle Cell Anemia]], [[Tay-Sachs Disease]], [[Phenylketonuria]].

Try it yourself. Why can a recessive disorder appear in a child of two healthy parents? Both parents are carriers and each passed on the recessive allele.
