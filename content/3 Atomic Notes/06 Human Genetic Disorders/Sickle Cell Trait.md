> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Sickle cell trait is the carrier state (Hb^A Hb^S): the person makes both normal and sickle hemoglobin, is usually healthy, and is resistant to malaria.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Carriers have half normal, half sickle hemoglobin: usually healthy and a poor home for the malaria parasite.

## Build it from first principles

Enough normal hemoglobin keeps most cells round; some may sickle under very low oxygen (high altitude, intense exercise, dehydration). The malaria parasite grows poorly in these cells.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Normal × carrier: chance of a child with sickle cell anemia?
> **Answer:** 0%; every child gets at least one Hb^A.
