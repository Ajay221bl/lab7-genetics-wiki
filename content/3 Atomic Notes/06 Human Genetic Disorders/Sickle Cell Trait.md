Sickle cell trait is the carrier state, Hbᴬ Hbˢ: the person makes both normal and sickle hemoglobin, is usually healthy, and resists malaria.

Enough normal hemoglobin keeps most cells round. Some can sickle under extreme low oxygen (high altitude, intense exercise, dehydration). And the malaria parasite grows poorly in these cells, which is the carriers' hidden advantage.

Try it yourself. Is a person with sickle cell trait "affected"? Usually not: they're a carrier.
