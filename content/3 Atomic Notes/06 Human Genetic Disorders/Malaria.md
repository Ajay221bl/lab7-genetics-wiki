> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Malaria is a disease caused by a Plasmodium parasite, spread by mosquitoes, that infects and destroys red blood cells.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> A parasite that lives in red blood cells; it's the selective pressure behind the sickle story.

## Build it from first principles

It explains why the sickle allele is common in parts of Africa, the Middle East and India: carriers survive malaria better ([[Heterozygote Advantage]]).

> [!question]- Test yourself: How does malaria keep Hb^S common?
> **Answer:** It kills more non-carriers, so carriers leave more children.
