> [!abstract] In one sentence
> An autosomal dominant disorder is caused by an allele on an autosome that causes disease even in one copy (Aa or AA).

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> One faulty copy is enough to cause trouble, like one bad ingredient spoiling the dish.

## Build it from first principles

An affected Aa parent with an unaffected aa partner has a **½ chance per child** of an affected child. An affected AA parent passes it to every child (study guide Q5c).

Examples (Q5b): [[Huntington Disease]], [[Marfan Syndrome]], [[Achondroplasia]], [[Neurofibromatosis]].

> [!question]- Test yourself: Affected Aa × unaffected aa: chance per child?
> **Answer:** ½.
