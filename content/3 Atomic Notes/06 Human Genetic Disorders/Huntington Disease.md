Huntington disease is an autosomal dominant disorder in which brain cells slowly die, usually starting in mid-life.

Because it often appears after people have had children, the allele can be passed on before anyone knows. It's the classic proof that dominant doesn't mean common: the allele is dominant **and** rare.

Try it yourself. An affected Aa parent and an unaffected partner: chance per child? ½.
