> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Huntington disease is an autosomal dominant disorder in which brain cells slowly die, usually starting in mid-life.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> A dominant allele with a delayed fuse: symptoms often start after people have had children.

## Build it from first principles

Because symptoms appear after people often have children, the dominant allele can be passed on before anyone knows. It shows that 'dominant' doesn't mean 'common'.

> [!question]- Test yourself: Inheritance pattern?
> **Answer:** Autosomal dominant.
