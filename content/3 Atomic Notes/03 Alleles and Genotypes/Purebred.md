A purebred (true-breeding) plant or animal is homozygous for a trait, so when bred with its own kind it always produces offspring with that same trait.

Self-pollinate purebred purple corn (RR) and every kernel is purple, generation after generation, because RR can only give R. Mendel began every experiment with purebreds, so he knew the parents' genotypes for certain. The [[P Generation|P generation]] of a classic cross is two purebreds: RR × rr.

Try it yourself. Is a purebred homozygous or heterozygous? Homozygous.
