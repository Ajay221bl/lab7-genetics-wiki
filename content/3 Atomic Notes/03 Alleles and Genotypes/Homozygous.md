> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Homozygous means carrying two identical alleles of a gene (for example RR or rr).

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Homozygous: both cards in your hand for this gene are the same.

## Build it from first principles

Slide 4: 'A diploid organism with two of the same alleles for a certain gene.' *Homo* = same; *zygous* comes from zygote.

Two kinds: [[Homozygous Dominant]] (RR) and [[Homozygous Recessive]] (rr). A homozygous parent can make only **one kind of gamete**, which makes its offspring predictable. Homozygous individuals 'breed true' (see [[Purebred]]).

> [!question]- Test yourself: How many kinds of gametes does a homozygous parent make?
> **Answer:** One.
