Homozygous means carrying two identical alleles of a gene, like RR or rr.

"Homo" means same. Both cards in your hand for this gene are the same card. The slides: "a diploid organism with two of the same alleles for a certain gene".

The useful consequence: a homozygous parent can make only **one kind** of gamete. RR only gives R; rr only gives r. That makes its offspring predictable, which is why homozygous plants are called [[Purebred|purebred]] or true-breeding.

Two kinds: [[Homozygous Dominant]] (RR) and [[Homozygous Recessive]] (rr).

Try it yourself. How many kinds of gametes does an hh plant make? One: h.
