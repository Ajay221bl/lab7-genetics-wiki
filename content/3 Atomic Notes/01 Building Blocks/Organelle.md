An organelle is a specialized structure inside a cell that does a particular job, like an organ inside a body.

Examples: the [[Nucleus|nucleus]] (stores DNA), [[Mitochondrion|mitochondria]] (make energy), chloroplasts in plants (do [[Photosynthesis|photosynthesis]]), ribosomes (build proteins).

Two organelles, mitochondria and chloroplasts, carry their own DNA, a clue that they were once free-living bacteria ([[Endosymbiotic Theory]]).

Try it yourself. Which organelle's DNA is used to trace the female line? The mitochondrion's.
