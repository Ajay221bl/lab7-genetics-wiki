A protein is a chain of amino acids, built from a gene's instructions, that folds into a shape that lets it do one job.

If genes are recipes, proteins are the cooks and the kitchen tools. They do almost all the work in a cell: [[Enzyme|enzymes]] run chemical reactions, [[Hemoglobin|hemoglobin]] carries oxygen, other proteins form structures or carry signals.

A protein's job depends on its shape, and its shape depends on the order of its [[Amino Acid|amino acids]], which depends on the gene. That's the full chain from DNA to trait.

Try it yourself. Trace the chain from the R allele to a purple kernel. R gene → working enzyme (a protein) → purple pigment → purple kernel.
