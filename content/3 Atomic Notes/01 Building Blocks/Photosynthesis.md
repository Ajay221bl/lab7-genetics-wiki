Photosynthesis is how plants use light energy, carbon dioxide and water to make sugar, their food.

It happens in chloroplasts, powered by light absorbed by [[Chlorophyll|chlorophyll]]. No chlorophyll, no photosynthesis, no food. That single chain explains the fate of the albino seedlings.

Try it yourself. Why do albino corn seedlings grow at first and then die? They live off food stored in the seed, but can't photosynthesize to make more.
