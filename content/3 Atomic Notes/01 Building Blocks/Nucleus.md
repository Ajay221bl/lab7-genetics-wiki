The nucleus is the membrane-wrapped compartment of a plant or animal cell that holds its chromosomes.

Think of the nucleus as the library where the master copy of the cookbook is kept. The recipes never leave; the cell makes working copies (RNA) to take out to the kitchen ([[Cytoplasm|cytoplasm]]) where proteins are built.

Cells with a nucleus are [[Eukaryote|eukaryotes]] (plants, animals, fungi). Bacteria have no nucleus ([[Prokaryote|prokaryotes]]).

Try it yourself. Where is the DNA that a corn kernel inherited from its pollen parent? In the nucleus of each of its cells, on the chromosomes from the sperm.
