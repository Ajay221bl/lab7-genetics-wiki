> [!abstract] In one sentence
> The seedling trays are two six-pack pots per table, about 24 corn seedlings in total, all from the same parental cross.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Your table's sample of one family of baby corn plants.

## Build it from first principles

TA meeting: each stalk is one plant; count the tiny ones too; trays are sometimes 23 or 25. One student per table counts the seedlings, at the table and for the whole room. Seedlings stay in their pots.

Example tray from the meeting: 18 green, 5 albino.

> [!question]- Test yourself: About how many plants per table?
> **Answer:** About 24.
