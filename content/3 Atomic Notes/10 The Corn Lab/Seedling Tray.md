The seedling tray is two six-pack pots per table, about 24 corn seedlings in total, all from the same parental cross.

TA meeting tips: each stalk is one plant; count the tiny ones too; trays are sometimes 23 or 25. One student per table counts. Seedlings stay in their pots. Example tray from the meeting: 18 green, 5 albino.

The question you answer with it: who were the parents? Test 3:1 (Gg × Gg) and 1:1 (Gg × gg). The tray gives χ² = 0.130 against 3:1 (keep) and 7.348 against 1:1 (reject), so the parents were Gg × Gg.

Try it yourself. Why can't the parents be GG × anything? Then no seedling could be gg.
