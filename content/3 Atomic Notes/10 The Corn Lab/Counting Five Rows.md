In Lab 7 each student counts the kernels in five rows of one ear (not the whole cob), giving a sample of about 100-250 kernels.

Taste a spoonful, not the whole pot. Five rows is a big enough [[Sample|sample]] for chi-square and leaves time for the math ([[Why we count five rows instead of the whole ear]]). On a short ear, 6 rows is fine. Count straight down the length; mark your starting row so you don't double-count.

The TA meeting deliberately avoids "count 100 kernels": messy totals force real expected-count math.

Try it yourself. About how many kernels should five rows give? 100-250.
