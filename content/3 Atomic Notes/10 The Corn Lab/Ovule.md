> [!abstract] In one sentence
> An ovule is the structure in a flower that contains the egg; once fertilized it develops into a seed (a corn kernel).

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> The ovule is the egg's capsule that becomes a seed.

## Build it from first principles

In corn every ovule on the ear has its own silk, so each kernel can have a different father in an open field, but in a controlled cross all share the same parents.

> [!question]- Test yourself: What does a fertilized corn ovule become?
> **Answer:** A kernel.
