An ovule is the structure on the ear that holds the egg; once fertilized, it grows into a kernel.

Ovule = the plant's egg holder. Pollen supplies the sperm; the ovule supplies the egg; together they make one kernel.

Try it yourself. What does a fertilized ovule become? A kernel.
