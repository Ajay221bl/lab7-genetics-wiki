A smooth kernel has at least one H allele (HH or Hh), so it turns sugar into starch and stays plump when it dries.

Starch is packed, so the kernel keeps its shape. See [[Why wrinkled kernels are sweet]].

Try it yourself. Genotypes of a smooth kernel? HH or Hh.
