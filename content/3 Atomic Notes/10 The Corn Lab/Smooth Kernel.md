> [!abstract] In one sentence
> A smooth (starchy) kernel has at least one H allele (HH or Hh): it stores more starch, loses less water as it dries, and keeps a full, smooth shape.

> [!example] Explain it like I'm new (the Feynman way)
> Starch holds its shape as the kernel dries, like a full sack of flour.

## Build it from first principles

Manual p. 131. This is field corn. On ears 3-5, H/h means smooth/wrinkled (in the pea problems H/h means tall/short — different gene!).

> [!question]- Test yourself: Possible genotypes?
> **Answer:** HH or Hh.
