Anthocyanin is the purple-red pigment that colors purple corn kernels (and blueberries, red cabbage and grapes).

The R allele leads to an enzyme step that makes anthocyanin in the kernel's outer layer. rr kernels skip it, and you see the yellow underneath. One R makes enough pigment, so purple is dominant.

Try it yourself. Genotype of a yellow kernel? rr.
