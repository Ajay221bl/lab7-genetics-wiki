The alternative hypothesis (H₁) is the opposite of the null: the gap between observed and expected is too large to be chance, so something other than the predicted ratio is going on.

If H₀ is "innocent", H₁ is "guilty". You only accept it when the evidence (a large χ²) is strong.

Try it yourself. Ear 1 gives χ² = 0.427 against 3:1. Which hypothesis wins? H₀: 0.427 is far below 3.841.
