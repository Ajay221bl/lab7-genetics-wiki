A retained null hypothesis is one you kept because χ² fell below the critical value; it's another way to say "failed to reject".

Retained, not crowned. It stays standing until better data comes along.

Try it yourself. Ear 1's χ² = 0.427. Is the 3:1 hypothesis retained? Yes.
