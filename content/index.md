# Lab 7 · Genetics and Patterns of Inheritance, from first principles

> [!example] The whole lab in eight sentences
> 1. A **gene** is a stretch of DNA that is a recipe for a protein, and proteins build traits.
> 2. You carry **two copies** of each gene, one from each parent.
> 3. The copies can be different versions, **alleles**; your pair is your **genotype**, what shows is your **phenotype**.
> 4. How two alleles combine into one phenotype is the **pattern of inheritance**.
> 5. **Meiosis** puts one allele of each gene into each egg or sperm, chosen by chance.
> 6. A **Punnett square** lists every egg against every sperm, predicting what fraction of offspring get each genotype.
> 7. Real offspring never match exactly, because **chance** decides which egg meets which sperm.
> 8. The **chi-square test** asks: is the gap between what we counted and what we predicted small enough to be chance?
